Comprehensions

List, set, dict, and generator comprehensions — concise ways to build collections.

Definition

Comprehensions let you build lists, sets, dicts, and generators in a single readable expression instead of a multi-line loop with append.

How to explain in interview:

“List comprehensions are syntactic sugar for map+filter. Generator expressions are lazy — they yield one item at a time and use far less memory for large ranges.”

Prefer comprehensions over manual loops when building collections — they are faster and more Pythonic.

Practice

  • Build a dict comprehension from a list of tuples
  • Compare memory: list vs generator with sys.getsizeof
  • Filter a list of names starting with "A"
